Static Oral History NLP Demo

Patterns in public Densho oral history transcripts.

This page is an honest, static demonstration inspired by the claim that large-scale NLP over Densho oral history transcripts can surface patterns in location, topics, and sentiment. It is not presented as an exact replication of Chen et al. unless the corpus size and methods happen to match exactly.

The visualizations below use a reproducible pipeline over publicly accessible Densho interview metadata and linked transcript files. In practice, Densho often exposes public transcripts as segmented HTML or PDF transcript files, so this demo reports the exact number of usable transcript segments and the number of interviews they came from.

Method and Limitations

What this build does, and what it does not claim

    Oral histories are complex, contextual narratives. Topic models compress that richness into a small number of keyword bundles, and sentiment tools can over-read quoted speech, trauma, irony, or historical framing. This demo is useful for pattern-finding and classroom discussion, not for replacing careful reading of the interviews themselves.
